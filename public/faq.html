Frequently Asked Questions
  • 1. What is CareerCounselling? ▶
    CareerCounselling is a student-focused career counselling platform developed as a college minor project. It helps students discover suitable career options using online assessments, expert guidance, and helpful resources.
  • 2. How does the career assessment test work? ▶
    Our career assessment test asks you a series of questions about your interests, skills, and preferences. Based on your answers, the platform suggests career paths that best match your profile.
  • 3. Is CareerCounselling free to use? ▶
    Yes, all core features of CareerCounselling-including assessments and career resources-are completely free for students, as this is an academic project.
  • 4. How can I connect with a career counselor or mentor? ▶
    After registering and logging in, you can view available counselors and mentors on the platform. You can book a session or send a message directly from your dashboard.
  • 5. How do I create an account on CareerCounselling? ▶
    Click on the "Register" button in the navigation bar, fill in your details, and submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email to activate your account.
  • 6. How is my data kept private and secure? ▶
    All user data is securely stored and is never shared with third parties. The platform follows standard privacy practices, and your information is used only for providing personalized career guidance.
  • 7. What should I do if I forget my password or cannot log in? ▶
    If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page to reset it. For other login issues, please contact our support team via the Contact page.
  • 8. Who developed CareerCounselling? ▶
    CareerCounselling was designed and developed by a team of undergraduate students as part of their college minor project. The project aims to help fellow students make informed career choices.
