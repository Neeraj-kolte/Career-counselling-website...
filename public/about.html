About CareerCounselling

CareerCounselling is a student-focused career counselling platform developed as a college minor project. Our aim is to help students make informed career decisions through modern technology, expert advice, and easy-to-use tools.

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to bridge the gap between students' aspirations and the right career path by providing personalized guidance, career resources, and interactive assessments. We want to make career planning accessible and engaging for every student.

Features

- Career assessment tests
- Career information and resources
- Mentor/counselor connect
- User-friendly dashboard
- AI-powered chatbot for instant answers
